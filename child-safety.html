Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 11 November 2026

thesixsevenapp, operated by Sixty IT Services Szolgáltató Korlátolt Felelősségű Társaság, has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and for child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

This page sets out our standards, how to report a concern, and how we respond.

How the app limits risk by design

thesixsevenapp is deliberately minimal, and that materially reduces the surface for abuse:

  • No free-text messaging. Users can only transmit two fixed words, "six" and "seven". It is not possible to write a message.
  • No images, video, audio, or file sharing of any kind. CSAM cannot be uploaded, stored, or transmitted through the app.
  • No profile photos. Avatars are chosen from a fixed set bundled in the app; nothing is uploaded.
  • No location sharing and no way to find users physically near you.
  • No dating features and no matching of strangers for romantic or sexual purposes.

The only text a user can author is a username, which is screened against a prohibited-terms list when it is created or changed.

What is prohibited

We prohibit, without exception:

  • Any use of the service involving the sexual abuse or exploitation of a child
  • Usernames that sexualise children, reference CSAM, or are used to signal or solicit it
  • Using the service to contact, groom, harass, or intimidate a child
  • Using the service to direct anyone to CSAE material or communities elsewhere

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over. Users confirm they meet that minimum age when registering, and the app is rated and distributed accordingly.

How to report a concern

In the app

Open the profile of the account you are concerned about, tap the menu in the top-right, and choose Report. Reports reach us directly. The person reported is not told who reported them.

You can also Block any account from the same menu, which immediately stops them contacting or following you.

By email

For child safety concerns, including anything you believe may involve CSAE, contact us directly at info@courtrent.com.

This address is monitored, and child safety reports are treated as urgent and prioritised above all other correspondence.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first. Reporting to us is not a substitute for contacting the police.

How we respond

When we receive a child safety report, we:

  1. Review it as a priority, ahead of other reports
  2. Take action on the account, which may include removing an offending username, suspending the account, or permanently terminating it
  3. Preserve relevant records where we are required or permitted to do so, to support any investigation
  4. Report to the appropriate authorities where the law requires it, or where we judge it necessary to protect a child

We cooperate with law enforcement and with relevant national and regional authorities, and comply with applicable child protection law in the jurisdictions where the app is available — including, in the European Union, reporting obligations under applicable EU and Hungarian law.

Accounts terminated for child safety reasons are not reinstated.

Point of contact

Our designated point of contact for child safety and CSAM prevention is reachable at info@courtrent.com.

Review

We review these standards as the app changes. Should we ever introduce features that increase risk — free-text messaging, image sharing, or anything that lets users meet strangers for personal purposes — these standards and our moderation capability will be revised before those features ship.
