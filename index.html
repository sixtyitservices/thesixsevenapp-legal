Privacy Policy

thesixsevenapp

Last updated: 4 November 2026

thesixsevenapp is operated by Sixty IT Services Szolgáltató Korlátolt Felelősségű Társaság ("we", "us"), a company registered in Hungary. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what control you have over it.

The short version: the app is deliberately minimal. You send one fixed word to someone, and they can send one fixed word back. There is no messaging, no photo upload, no location, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking of any kind. We collect the smallest amount of data needed to make accounts and that exchange work.

What we collect

DataWhy
Email address Identifies your account and lets you sign back in. Stored only in Firebase Authentication — it is never written into our database and is never shown to other users. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your address, we only ever receive Apple's private relay address.
Username How other people find and identify you. Public by design.
Avatar choice A reference to one of a fixed set of cartoon images bundled in the app. No image is ever uploaded, and no photo of you is involved.
Privacy setting Whether your profile is private, which controls who can see your activity.
Word exchanges Who sent a word to whom, the type of word, and when. This is the core function of the app.
Follows and blocks Who follows whom, pending follow requests, and who you have blocked.
Notification token A device identifier issued by Firebase Cloud Messaging, used to send you push notifications. Only stored if you allow notifications.
Age confirmation A single yes/no that you confirmed you are 13 or older. We do not store your date of birth or your age.

What we deliberately do not collect

  • No real names, dates of birth, phone numbers, or postal addresses
  • No photographs or uploaded files of any kind
  • No location data
  • No contacts or address book access
  • No analytics, behavioural tracking, advertising identifiers, or cross-app tracking
  • No payment or card details — purchases are handled entirely by Apple and Google, and we never see your payment information

How your data is used

We use the data above only to operate the app: to create and secure your account, to deliver words between users, to build your activity feed and notifications, to enforce follow and block settings, and to prevent abuse.

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to build profiles about you.

What other people can see

  • Your username, avatar, follower and following counts, and word totals are visible to anyone using the app.
  • If your profile is public, your activity and your follower/following lists are visible to anyone.
  • If your profile is private, those are visible only to followers whose request you have accepted.
  • Your email address is never visible to other users, under any setting.

Who processes your data

We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, and Cloud Messaging) to run the app. Google acts as our data processor under contract. Depending on how you sign in, Apple or Google also act as independent identity providers.

Our Firebase project is hosted in Google's nam5 multi-region in the United States. If you are in the European Economic Area, this means your data is transferred outside the EEA. Google provides for these transfers through the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

Your account data is kept for as long as your account exists.

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app. When you do:

  • Your authentication record, including your email address, is deleted.
  • Your username is released and your notification token is erased.
  • If you signed in with Apple, we revoke the authorization token Apple issued to us.
  • Records of past word exchanges are kept, but no longer identify you — you appear to other users only as "Deleted User". We keep these because they are part of other people's history, which would otherwise be destroyed along with your account.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 13, and you must confirm you are 13 or older when registering. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Your rights

If you are in the EEA or the UK, the GDPR gives you the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data, to restrict or object to how it is used, and to withdraw consent for notifications at any time (from your device settings).

You can exercise the most common of these directly in the app: usernames and avatars are editable in your profile, and account deletion is available in profile settings. For anything else, contact us using the details below.

You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority. In Hungary this is the Nemzeti Adatvédelmi és Információszabadság Hatóság (NAIH).

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored on Google's infrastructure. Access to our database is restricted by server-side security rules, and sensitive operations run through server-side functions rather than directly from the app. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit risk primarily by collecting as little as possible in the first place.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
