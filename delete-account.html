Delete your account

How to delete your thesixsevenapp account, and exactly what happens to your data. thesixsevenapp is published by Sixty IT Services Szolgáltató Korlátolt Felelősségű Társaság.

Delete it yourself, in the app

This takes effect immediately and needs no request or waiting period.

  1. Open thesixsevenapp and sign in.
  2. Tap your avatar in the top-left corner to open your profile.
  3. Tap the edit (pencil) icon in the top-right.
  4. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  5. Confirm when prompted.

If you signed in with Apple, you'll be asked to authenticate with Apple once more. This is so we can revoke the access token Apple issued to us, which is part of removing your account properly.

If you can't sign in

If you've lost access to your account and can't delete it from inside the app, email us and we'll delete it for you:

info@courtrent.com

Include the username or the email address on the account. We may ask a question to confirm the account is yours before deleting it. We aim to action requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Your email address and sign-in credentials Deleted immediately. The authentication record is destroyed, and you can no longer sign in.
Your username Removed immediately and released, so it becomes available for someone else to use.
Your notification token Deleted immediately. No further push notifications can be sent to your device.
Your avatar selection and privacy setting No longer associated with any usable account.
Sign in with Apple authorisation Revoked with Apple, if you used that sign-in method.

What is kept, and for how long

Records of the words exchanged between you and other people are kept, along with the follow relationships connected to them. These records no longer identify you: your username is gone, and you appear to other users only as "Deleted User".

We keep them because they are part of other people's history. Deleting them would remove words those users sent and received, from accounts that still exist.

Retention period: these anonymised records are retained indefinitely for as long as the service operates. They contain no email address, no username, and no contact details — only an internal identifier that is no longer linked to any account you can access.

If you want these records removed as well, email info@courtrent.com and say so explicitly. If you are in the EEA or the UK, you have a right to erasure under the GDPR, and we will act on that request.

Deleting some data without closing your account

You don't have to delete your whole account to have data removed. Email info@courtrent.com describing what you want deleted — for example specific word exchanges, your follow relationships, or your notification history — and we will action it within 30 days. Your account stays active.

This is available to everyone, wherever you live, not only to users covered by the GDPR.

Deleting the app

Removing thesixsevenapp from your device does not delete your account. Use one of the methods above.

Deleting your account also does not refund your purchase. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google, depending on where you bought the app.
