Common questions

How does the app work?

You send one fixed word to another user. They can send one fixed word back. That's the whole app — there is no free-text messaging.

How do I find someone?

Use the Search tab to look them up by username, or the Send tab to search and send in one step.

Why can't I see someone's activity?

Their profile is private. Send a follow request, and once they accept it you'll be able to see their activity, followers, and following. Public profiles are visible to everyone without following.

I sent a follow request and nothing happened.

Requests to private accounts stay pending until that person accepts or declines. They'll see it in their notifications.

How do I change my username or avatar?

Tap your avatar in the top-left corner to open your profile, then tap the edit icon. Usernames must be unique, and use only lowercase letters, numbers, and underscores.

How do I make my profile private or public?

Same place — profile, then edit, then the privacy toggle. New accounts are private by default.

How do I block someone?

Open their profile and choose Block from the menu in the top-right. They won't be able to send you words or follow you, any existing follow between you is removed, and they won't appear in your search results. They are not told that you blocked them.

How do I remove a follower?

Open your own profile, tap your follower count, and use the remove icon next to the person you want to remove.

I'm not getting notifications.

Check that notifications are enabled for thesixsevenapp in your device settings. On iOS that's Settings › Notifications; on Android it's Settings › Apps › Notifications.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app: open your profile, tap the edit icon, then Delete account.

This removes your sign-in details and releases your username. Records of past word exchanges remain visible to the people you exchanged them with, but no longer identify you — you appear as "Deleted User". This is explained in full in our Privacy Policy.

Deleting your account does not refund your purchase. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google, depending on where you bought the app.

Refunds and billing

thesixsevenapp is a one-time purchase, and there are no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Because the stores handle all payments, we cannot issue refunds ourselves — please contact Apple Support or Google Play Support.

Privacy

You can also ask us to delete specific data without closing your account — email info@courtrent.com and say what you'd like removed.

See our Privacy Policy for what we collect and why. In short: a username, an avatar choice, your word exchanges, and an email address used only for signing in. No tracking, no ads, no analytics.
